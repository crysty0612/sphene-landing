AEGIS 4-ZONE ARCHITECTURE

Hardware-Grade Vault Partitions

Unlike flat folder structures where AI agents have unrestricted access to all your notes, Sphene enforces kernel-level permission boundaries across 4 dedicated zones:

💼 Collaborative

Workspace/

Your active thinking space. Read and write access for you; collaborative staged read/write access for autonomous AI agents.

🛡️ Differential Timeline Guard

Agent edits are staged as diffs for your explicit human review with 1-click accept or reject.

📚 Write-Locked

Reference/

Permanent ground truth: curated literature, books, papers, and verified facts. Full write access for humans.

🔒 AI Write-Lock Enforced

AI agents have read-only access. Bots are mathematically barred from altering your source literature.

🔒 Hardware-Sealed

Private/

Personal reflections, journals, finances, and private research. Completely locked and encrypted on disk—strictly blocked from all AI access.

🚫 AI Agents Blocked (403)

Zero agent access. The kernel returns 403 Forbidden to any AI query targeting this partition.
