Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to common questions about our plans and collaboration.

Is Sphene completely free for individual use?

Yes. The entire Sphene core engine, sub-millisecond search index, client-side encryption, 3D knowledge graph, built-in visual tools, and agent MCP integrations are 100% free and open-format for local self-hosting. There are no trial periods, artificial document limits, or locked features for solo usage.

How does the Teams Collaboration Free Community Trial work?

Every copy of the Sphene app comes preloaded with the community license key freeSpheneSync. This grants instant access to create 1 shared team workspace, sync up to 150 documents (over 500,000 words of rich encrypted text), and connect teammates instantly via QR code or code pairing with a 30-second sync cadence—all backed by high-durability S3 storage at zero cost.

Can Sphene or the storage provider read my shared team notes?

Absolutely not. Sphene utilizes strict device-level zero-knowledge encryption. When you create or join a team, your device derives an encryption key from your team's secret passphrase. All notes are encrypted and decrypted locally on your phone or laptop. Only participants who possess the team passphrase or scanned the team QR code have the cryptographic ability to read the notes.

Do my teammates need to purchase a license to join my team?

No. Teammates and family members can join an existing team room with zero license required. They simply scan the team QR code or enter the Room Code and Passphrase to start collaborating immediately.

Is there any vendor lock-in?

Zero. Every note is saved as a plain Markdown file with standard YAML frontmatter directly in your filesystem. You can inspect, edit, or migrate your notes with any text editor or tool at any time.
