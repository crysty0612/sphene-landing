Frequently Asked Questions

Is Sphene completely free to use?

Yes. The entire Sphene core engine, sub-millisecond search index, client-side encryption, built-in visual tools, and agent MCP integrations are 100% free and open-format for local self-hosting. There are no trial periods, artificial document limits, or locked features.

Will there ever be paid cloud services?

If hosted synchronization or managed backup relay services are explored in the future, they will be strictly optional conveniences. The core self-hosted software will always remain 100% free and fully functional on your own hardware.

Is there any vendor lock-in?

Zero. Every note is saved as a plain Markdown file with standard YAML frontmatter directly in your filesystem. You can inspect, edit, or migrate your notes with any text editor or tool at any time.
