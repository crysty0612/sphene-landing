Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use Sphene completely free of charge?

Yes. The complete Sphene Knowledge Hub engine, full local Aegis encryption, the Hermes Agent skill, interactive graph view, and desktop PWA standalone apps are 100% free and open-format. You can self-host forever with zero restrictions on notes or volume.

How does Zero-Knowledge Cloud Relay work?

Your notes are encrypted directly on your local device with your secret passphrase using AES-256-CBC PBKDF2 before ever leaving your hardware. The Sphene Pro relay server only routes and mirrors opaque encrypted ciphertext. Even if our servers are compromised, attackers obtain only unreadable binary noise.

What happens if I cancel my Pro subscription?

You keep all of your notes and data. Because Sphene stores files as standard Markdown on your drive, you can continue accessing your entire second brain locally, migrate to another machine, or use standard text editors without paying a cent.

Is there any vendor lock-in?

Zero. Unlike proprietary cloud note-taking apps that trap your thoughts inside closed databases, Sphene guarantees digital permanence. Every note is plain Markdown with standard YAML frontmatter legible in 40 years.
