Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use Sphene completely free of charge?

Yes. The complete Sphene Knowledge Hub engine, full local Aegis encryption, the Hermes Agent skill, interactive graph view, and desktop PWA standalone apps are 100% free and open-format. You can self-host forever with zero restrictions on notes or volume.

Why can't I buy Sphene Pro today?

We refuse to take user payments until our zero-knowledge cloud relay has completed rigorous security and stress audits. You can use the local engine for free today, and email us if you'd like to test early relay builds.

How will billing work when Sphene Pro launches?

When Sphene Pro is released, billing will be handled via Stripe (supporting Apple Pay, Google Pay, credit cards, and SEPA) with a self-serve billing portal and 1-click cancellations.

Is there any vendor lock-in?

Zero. Unlike proprietary cloud note-taking apps that trap your thoughts inside closed databases, Sphene guarantees digital permanence. Every note is plain Markdown with standard YAML frontmatter legible in 40 years.
