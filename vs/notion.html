The Case for Local Sovereignty

1. Never Lose Access When the Internet Goes Down

When Notion suffers an outage or when you're on a flight, train, or remote connection, your notes become unreachable. Sphene is architected from day one as a local-first system. The web interface is a standalone Progressive Web App (PWA) cached directly in your browser. All your notes, knowledge graphs, and mathematical formulas render in milliseconds without ever waiting for a remote server response.

2. Uncompromised Privacy: Zero Cookies, Zero Telemetry

Notion monitors user actions to optimize enterprise monetization, deploying analytics beacons and tracking cookies. Sphene believes your private second brain is sacred. We operate with a strict Zero-Cookie and Zero-Telemetry guarantee. We don't track what you write, how many notes you have, or what tools you use. In fact, our presentation website even self-hosts all fonts locally to prevent external IP address leakage under European GDPR standards.

3. Native AI Agent Freedom via Model Context Protocol (MCP)

Notion gates AI behind an expensive monthly subscription that only talks to Notion's selected cloud models. In Sphene, your second brain becomes a sovereign knowledge kernel for any autonomous AI agent you choose. Through native Model Context Protocol (MCP), tools like Claude Desktop, Cursor, and Hermes Agent connect to your notes via typed, deterministic APIs—with interactive human-veto diff review to keep you in total control.
