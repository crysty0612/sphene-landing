THE TRIANGLE OF TRUTH

Built Without the Compromises of Legacy PKMs

Most note-taking apps force you to choose between slow Electron memory bloat or closed proprietary cloud silos. Sphene delivers all three: Light, Fast, and Secure.

🪶
<25MB RAM

LIGHT: Zero Electron Bloat

The Problem: Apps like Obsidian rely on Chromium and Node runtimes, consuming 800MB–2GB of RAM just to edit text notes.

The Sphene Advantage: Sphene is a compiled native engine running quietly in the background consuming <25MB of RAM—whisper-quiet on any laptop, homelab, or workstation.

⚡
<2ms Latency

FAST: Instant Sub-Millisecond Search

The Problem: As vaults exceed thousands of notes, JavaScript event loops and Dataview queries lag and freeze your workspace.

The Sphene Advantage: Sphene embeds an SQLite relational index with FTS5 search. Queries and [[Wikilink]] lookups execute in under 2 milliseconds (<180µs benchmarked).

🛡️
100% Sovereign

SECURE: Plaintext Markdown & Encryption

The Problem: Unsandboxed community plugins can read your entire drive, while notes sit in unencrypted plaintext or closed cloud databases.

The Sphene Advantage: Notes live on your physical disk in standard plaintext Markdown with optional AES-256 encryption. Extensions run in cryptographically verified sandboxes.

AI AGENT HARMONY

Designed from the Ground Up for Autonomous AI

Connecting AI agents shouldn't mean letting bots silently corrupt your knowledge. Sphene gives agents instant memory recall while keeping you in total control.

🔌

Native Agent Gateway (MCP & REST/SSE)

Connect Claude Desktop, Cursor, or Hermes Agent in seconds via official Model Context Protocol (MCP) over stdio, local sockets, and change streams.

⏱️

The Differential Timeline ("Human Veto")

Never fear AI hallucinating or overwriting your notes. Agent edits are cleanly staged at the block level for your visual review with 1-click accept or rollback.

🗄️

Hardware-Grade Vault Partitions

Strict architectural boundaries: Workspace/ (collaborative human & agent read/write), Reference/ (human-curated read-only for agents), and Private/ (sealed with AES-256-GCM, zero agent access).

📦

Zero-Trust Sandboxed Extensions

Lightweight WebAssembly isolates consuming <3MB RAM per worker. Enforces declarative capability manifests (path whitelisting, domain-restricted network, zero shell access).

📊

Sub-ms Relational Index (No Dataview)

Replaces slow client-side parsing with an embedded, low-latency relational engine. Agents execute structured queries via /api/v1/query without wasting LLM context tokens.

🕸️

Interactive 3D Knowledge Graph

Visual force-directed knowledge graph rendered directly from the engine. Smooth traversal across [[Wikilinks]], tags, and backlinks without dragging down browser performance.
